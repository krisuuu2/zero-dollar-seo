# Workflow: progress report

Run `python scripts/gsc_pull.py` and `python scripts/ga4_pull.py` (if configured). Then write `reports/progress-<date>.md` and summarise it in the chat:
- Clicks and impressions per month for the last 12 months, and the change since the setup date in `reports/baseline.md`, as multiples ("×2.1") and percentages.
- Visits from AI assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot) per month.
- The 5 articles from `content/ledger.md` that grew the most, and the 3 that did nothing yet.
- What the numbers mean in one paragraph, and the one thing to focus on next.
Never round a result up or present a short spike as a trend; say how many weeks of data a conclusion is based on.
